Your ads × your flow

Three promises of a free listing. One first launch for all of them.

Your YouTube videos promise “بيع سيارتك مجاناً اليوم”, “Sell anything for free” and “بيع عقارك مجاناً اليوم”. Your Google and Meta ads add “Your First Listing is Free” and “أول إعلان مجاني!”. Your money comes after that: Premium, Turbo, Repost and VIP on a listing that is already live.

Flow Builder can open a different first launch by the campaign an install came from, so the first screens keep that ad’s promise, the listing goes live free, and promotion is offered on the seller’s own listing. Each ad is then judged on the promotions its installs buy, not on installs.

Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the YouTube, Google or Meta campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Cars, any item and property each get their own first screens; installs from your brand search and other ads keep today’s start.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on listings published, promotions bought and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas, in Arabic first and in English. OpenSooq sells promotion on listings, not subscriptions, so the “paywall” here is the promotion sheet a seller meets once their free listing is live. Your Saudi App Store lists ten packages from 9.99 to 59.99 ر.س without saying which one buys what, so no screen ties a price to a product. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
